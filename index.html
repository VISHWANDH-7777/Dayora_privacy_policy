1. Overview

Dayora is a birthday and task reminder app. We aim to collect only the information needed for the app's features and to explain clearly how that information is handled.

Important: This template assumes birthdays, tasks, and reminder details are stored on the user's device and are not uploaded to a server. Before publishing, verify this against the released app and remove or revise any statement that is not accurate.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, you may enter information such as:

  • Names, birthday dates, and optional notes or relationships.
  • Task titles, descriptions, dates, times, and reminder preferences.
  • App preferences, such as theme settings.

If the app uses local storage only, this information remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it using a feature provided by your device or another app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may remove locally stored information.

Do not enter information about another person unless you have a legitimate reason to do so.

3. Permissions and notifications

Dayora may ask for notification permission to deliver birthday and task reminders. You can manage notification permissions in your device's system settings. If permission is denied, reminders may not appear as notifications.

If a future or current version requests other permissions, those permissions should be used only for the feature described to you in the app.

4. Sharing and third-party services

Under the local-only design described in this policy, Dayora does not send your birthday or task entries to a Dayora-operated cloud database and does not sell your personal information.

Your device platform or any third-party libraries included in the app may process limited technical information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Check the actual app dependencies and network behavior before publishing this statement. If you add analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud backup, account login, or other online services, update this policy to identify what data is collected, why it is used, and the relevant service providers.

If you use your device's share functionality, information you choose to share may be sent to the app or person you select.

5. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information is generally kept until you delete it within the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to how the released version implements storage and backups.

If a future version stores information on external servers, this section must explain how to request deletion of that server-held information.

6. Children's privacy

Dayora is not specifically directed at children under 13. The app's actual target audience and applicable age requirements may vary by country. If the app is intended for children or collects information from children, this policy and the app must be updated to comply with applicable laws and Google Play Families requirements.

7. Security

We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and installing app and operating-system updates. Local storage does not automatically mean that data is encrypted. Do not assume information is encrypted unless the app has implemented and verified appropriate encryption.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Dayora's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page, with an updated effective date.
